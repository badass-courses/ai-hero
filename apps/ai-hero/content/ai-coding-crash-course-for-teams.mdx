## Give your team a shared way to build with AI.

Turn individual experiments into a process your engineers can use together. Matt Pocock's AI Coding Loop is a self-paced course built around hands-on practice, and every seat is a full licence.

### A shared process

1. **Grill.** Reach shared understanding before anyone writes code.
2. **Spec.** Make the work clear enough that an agent can be held to it.
3. **Tickets.** Break it into tasks with a definition of done.
4. **Implement.** Build to your standards, with the agent doing the typing.
5. **Review.** Keep your judgment in the loop.

Your engineers. Your standards. A process you can share.

## Different starting points. A common approach.

Some teammates improvise. Others chase speed. Some don't trust agents at all. The course builds a common approach without asking anyone to outsource their judgment.

**Align before you build.** Grilling surfaces hidden assumptions and gets your teammates and your agent to the same understanding of the problem.

**Put your standards into practice.** Work through the full loop, then use skills and steering documents to point the agent at your own coding standards instead of its defaults.

**Understand what is happening.** Inspect the requests your harness sends to the model. Practise managing context and reviewing generated code, so the team takes control instead of guessing.

## A few practical questions

**Do we need to learn at the same time?** No. The course is self-paced. Each person works through the lessons on their own schedule, and you assign seats from your account whenever you are ready.

**Do we use our production code?** The course includes a shared practice codebase. Your team then applies the techniques to its own projects.

**Do we all need Claude Code?** Matt teaches with Claude Code, and both Claude Code and Codex are supported in the material. The focus is the workflow, not one tool.

**Can we get an invoice?** Yes. Buy seats online with a card, or ask about team access below for invoicing and procurement.
