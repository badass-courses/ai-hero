Finer globe coordinates are projections of GeoNames dumps.

US ZIP centroids: GeoNames US postal codes
http://download.geonames.org/export/zip/US.zip

City and region centroids: GeoNames cities with population >= 15,000
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
plus admin1CodesASCII.txt for subdivision names.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://www.geonames.org/

Country centroids remain a world-countries 5.1.0 projection. See CENTROIDS-LICENSE.txt.

This directory stores cca2, postal, city, region, latitude, and longitude only.
